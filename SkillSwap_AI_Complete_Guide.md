## 1. What is SkillSwap AI?

**SkillSwap AI** is a online peer-to-peer marketplace where college students and tech enthusiasts can **teach skills they know** and **learn new skills for free** using a credit-based system.

Instead of paying high fees for online courses or bootcamps, students trade knowledge like currency:
- **Example:** If you know React.js, you can teach a junior student for 1 hour ➔ You earn 15 credits. You can then spend those 15 credits to get 1-on-1 mentorship on Artificial Intelligence or Spring Boot from a senior student.

## 3. How SkillSwap AI Works (Step-by-Step)

1. **Step 1: Sign Up**
   Create a free account using your college email address. You instantly receive **50 free starter credits** in your wallet.

2. **Step 2: Find a Skill or AI Roadmap**
   Search for a skill (like Java, Python, or Figma Design) or use the **AI Roadmap Tool** to generate a custom 4-week step-by-step learning plan.

3. **Step 3: Book a Mentorship Session**
   Pick a date and time to book a 1-on-1 video mentorship session with a student mentor.

4. **Step 4: Earn Credits by Teaching**
   To replenish your credit balance, list skills you are good at and mentor junior students.

5. **Step 5: Get Verified Completion Certificates**
   After completing a session, receive a digital completion certificate featuring a unique verification code to share on LinkedIn & GitHub.

## 4. Key Features & AI Capabilities

| Feature | What It Does (In Simple Words) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **AI Learning Roadmap** | Type any skill (e.g. PyTorch), and AI builds a step-by-step 4-week learning curriculum with weekly milestones. |
| **AI Mentor Matcher** | AI analyzes your learning goals and recommends top-rated student mentors in your university. |
| **Credit Escrow System** | Credits stay locked safely during booking and are transferred to the mentor only when the session successfully finishes. |
| **Session Chat** | Direct messaging thread between learner and mentor to prepare before the video call. |
| **Verified Certificates** | Cryptographically signed digital certificate with a verification code (`CERT-SKILLSWAP-123`). |
| **Admin Analytics** | Admin dashboard to manage users, suspend spam accounts, and audit platform transactions. |

---

## 5. How Real Money & Payouts Work

1. **Buying Credits (Razorpay)**: Students who do not want to teach can buy credit packs (e.g. ₹599 for 120 credits) using Google Pay, PhonePe, UPI, or Credit Cards. The real money goes directly into your bank account.
2. **Mentor Payouts**: Mentors who earn credits by teaching can cash out their credits for real money (e.g. 100 credits = ₹500) directly into their UPI ID or bank account.
